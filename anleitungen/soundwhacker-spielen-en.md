## What is it about?

**SoundWhacker Smart Control** turns the SoundWhackers (SW) into a musical instrument. Depending on the settings, each of the five SWs plays a chord, a note, a melody, an arpeggio, improvises or switches a loop. This way everyone can make music together, even without any previous knowledge.

It also works without SoundWhackers: on a computer the **keys 1–5** play the five SWs.

**Tip:** Tap **🌐 English** on the start page (next to “❓ Help”) to switch the whole app to English. **🌐 Deutsch** switches back.

## Setup (once)

1. On the **SW-HUB** choose **Keyboard** under *Settings → Bluetooth mode*. The HUB restarts.
2. On the **iPad**: *Settings → Bluetooth* → pair the HUB whose name ends in **-KEY**.
3. Open the app in **Safari**, tap **“Share” → “Add to Home Screen”**. Now it starts like a normal app.
4. **Tap once** so the sound starts. The first time, the instruments are loaded (about 21 MB) – after that the app also works **offline**.

> Note: control via **BLE-MIDI** does not work in Safari on the iPad. Use the **keyboard mode** there.

## Quick start

1. **Open the app** – click “Open app” at the top.
2. Tap the screen once.
3. Hit a SoundWhacker (or press the keys **1–5** on the PC) – done.

With **▼ Show melody and SW buttons** additional buttons appear:

- **▷ Melody 1–5** – start melodies one by one; **▶ Start all melodies** starts all of them.
- **🔇 SW1–SW5 / All** – mute single SWs or all of them.
- **↻** – set all running arpeggios back to step 1 (synchronise).

## Set up SoundWhackers

At the top you choose the SW (**SW1–SW5**). For each SW there are five ways of playing, switched on and off with one tap:

| Way of playing | What the SW does |
|---|---|
| ♫ **Chord** | Plays a chord as long as it is held |
| ♩ **Note** | Plays a single note |
| ♪ **Melody** | Plays its own melody or an arpeggio, step by step |
| ♬ **Impro** | Plays a random note of a scale with every hit – always sounds right |
| ↻ **Looper** | Switches a loop channel: SW1 controls channel 1, SW2 channel 2 … |

With **▼** you open the settings of each way of playing. With **▶ Preview** you test the sound.

### Entering a melody

1. In **♪ Melody ▼** choose the note with **◀ ▶**.
2. Tap the **note value** (1/1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, dotted or triplet). **▬ Rest** adds a rest.
3. **✓ Step +** adds the note. With **Auto: ON** this happens automatically.
4. Mistake? **⌫ Delete last** or **✕ Delete all**.
5. With **🎵 Arpeggio: ON** the melody is played in a loop like an arpeggio.

With **📋 Copy melody** you take over a melody from another SW.

### Improvising

Choose the key (C to B), the **scale** (major scale, pentatonic, minor pentatonic, Phrygian dominant) and the octave. With **⏱ On the beat** the notes land exactly on the next eighth note of the running loops or melodies.

### Looper mode

Choose the **channel** and whether the loop only sounds **👌 while held** or is switched **🔁 on / off** with every hit.

## Shared Melody

All SWs play one song together – **no matter which SW is pressed, it is always the next note**. Songs come from the Tischharfe app (also your own songs recorded there), e.g. “Ode to Joy”, “Silent Night”, “Happy Birthday”. Every SW sounds with its own instrument.

- **🔁 Any SW plays the melody** – every hit plays the next note.
- **🔢 Fixed SW order** – always SW1 → SW2 → … – whoever is not next stays silent.
- **🎯 Fixed SW notes** (⭐ songs only) – every SW always plays its own note. With **👀 Show whose turn it is** the SW that is next lights up.

With **🏠 Home** the Shared Melody keeps running; only **⏹ Stop** in the yellow notice ends it.

## Assisted Play & chord pad

For players who need support:

- **♫ Slots** – prepare several chords for **SW1**. With every hit SW1 plays the currently selected chord.
- **♫ Chord pad** – large chord buttons on the screen to tap.
  - **↔ Glide** – stroke across the buttons with your finger.
  - **👃 Head** – your nose moves a pointer over the pad (camera). If it rests on a button briefly, the chord sounds. **Dwell** (instant to 2 s) and **Movement** (large, medium, small) adjust this; **⊕ Center** takes the current head position as the centre.
  - **✎ Position** – move and arrange the buttons; **⬤** changes their shape.
- **🏠 Home** takes you back to the start page.

## Looper

Five loop channels that run together in time.

- **▶ Start / ■ Stop**, tempo with **− / +**
- **🔊 Preview all** – hear all channels at once (key 0)
- **Match all** – bring all loops to the same tempo
- **Loop generator**: set style, tempo, key and *dark ↔ bright* and *soft ↔ punchy*, then **🎲 Generate new**. With **💾 Save WAV files as ZIP** you can keep the loops.

## Arpeggio Maker

Creates an arpeggio over five chords with one instrument and loads it into the looper as five loops.

1. Choose an instrument.
2. Set the **pattern** (up, down, up and down, Alberti …, or guitar strumming and picking styles), **note value** (eighths or sixteenths), **octaves** and **register**.
3. **🎹 Create 5 arpeggio loops**.

## Settings

- **Instruments**: use the built-in sounds or **📁 Load your own SoundFont (.sf2)** – on the iPad preferably under 30–40 MB. With **+ Instrument** add more sounds to the list.
- **⌨ Note names** show/hide.
- **Control**: **Keys 1–5** or **BLE-MIDI** (not in Safari on the iPad).
- **🗑 Delete all settings** – resets the app to the default values.

## Presets

Settings are saved in this browser. Under **💾 Presets** you can **save** and **load** different settings under a name. With **⇓ Export / ⇑ Import** you back them up or move them to another device – preset files from the SW-HUB can be imported too.

## Tips & problems

- **No sound?** Tap the screen once. On the iPad check the silent mode (bell in Control Center) and the volume.
- **SW does not react?** Check that the HUB is in **keyboard mode** and that the HUB was paired with **-KEY**.
- **To test on the PC** simply press the keys 1–5.
