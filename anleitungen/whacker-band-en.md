## What is it about?

Five friendly figures stand on a stage. Each one holds a **SoundWhacker** and has its own colour: **red, purple, yellow, green, blue**. First the **band plays a song for you**, then the **participants play along live** – with real SoundWhackers or the keys 1–5.

Made for **inclusive music making** on a PC, iPad or tablet: large figures, strong colours, high contrast. Everyone simply remembers “I’m the green one”.

**Tip:** Tap **🌐 English** at the top of the app to switch the whole app to English. **🌐 Deutsch** switches back.

## Quick start

1. **Open the app** and tap the screen once (this turns the sound on).
2. The app always starts in **🙌 Play along → 🎯 Fixed SW notes / chords → 🎶 Everyone, any time**.
3. Choose a **song** – the default is “Ode to Joy” (5 notes).
4. Touch your SoundWhacker – your figure plays. The figure that is **next already glows softly**.
5. Want to hear the song first? **▶ Band plays** → **▶ Start** (or the space bar).

## How a figure plays

The SoundWhacker is a white tube with **two black rings** – one at the top, one at the bottom. The left hand holds the lower ring. To play, the right hand reaches for the **upper ring** – at that moment the **tube lights up in the figure’s colour** and the note sounds.

## Controls

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Play figure 1–5 | SoundWhacker 1–5, **keys 1–5** or tap the figure |
| Start / stop (Band plays) | **▶ Start** button or the **space bar** |
| Start the song again | **↺ From the start** – in the bar and, in full screen, at the bottom left |
| Tempo | Slider from **40 %** (“slow, for practice”) via **100 %** (“normal”) up to 130 % |
| Formation | **Row, Semicircle** (default), **Circle** or **Open U** – the figures walk smoothly to their new places |
| Sound | Sorted into **groups**: 🎹 Keyboards (piano, electric piano) – 🎶 Mallets (marimba, xylophone, vibraphone, music box, kalimba, steel drums) – 🎸 Guitars & plucked strings (guitar, harp, koto, distorted guitars) – 🎺 Wind instruments (flutes, clarinet, saxophones) – 🥁 Drums – ✨ Noises & effects |
| Drums | 6 kits: **Standard, Rock, Electronic, 808, Jazz, Brushes** – see “Drums” |
| Noises | **✨ Noises & effects** – see “Noises & effects” |
| Reverb | Slider from **0 %** (dry) via small room and hall up to **church** (100 %) |
| Full screen | **⛶ Full screen**: only the note band and the stage – the figures become as large as possible. Choose song, sound and way of playing first. Back with **✕ Exit full screen** at the bottom right (on the PC also **Esc**). In full screen nothing zooms or moves by accident. On iPad/iPhone Safari’s bar stays visible – **without the bar:** Share → **“Add to Home Screen”** and start the app from there. |

**Note length:** a note sounds as long as the SoundWhacker (or the key, the figure, the piano key) is **held** – at least a quarter of a second. When the band plays, every note lasts as long as its written note.

Above the stage runs a **note band** of coloured dots: every dot is a note in the colour of the figure that plays it. When playing along, the song only continues when the right figure has played. At the end of the song it starts again from the beginning.

## Play along: four ways of playing

In **🙌 Play along** a second bar appears with four ways of playing – just like “Shared Melody” in SoundWhacker Smart Control:

| Way of playing | How it works |
|---|---|
| 🔁 **Any SW plays melody / chords** | Every touch – **no matter which SoundWhacker** – plays the next note of the song. The player’s figure moves and lights up. Because everyone may play, there are **no dots** at the top – the stage is larger instead. Only for **chord progressions** the dots with the chord names stay. |
| 🔢 **Fixed SW order** | In turn: SW1 → SW2 → SW3 … Whoever is next **already glows softly**. Whoever is not next stays silent (the figure briefly shakes its head). With **Players: SW1–2 … SW1–5** you set how many play along – the other figures fade. |
| 🎯 **Fixed SW notes / chords** | Every SoundWhacker has its **own note** (red = lowest … blue = highest). The figure that is next **already glows softly**. **Who may play?** – 🎯 *Only whose turn it is*: only that figure sounds, the others stay silent. 🎶 *Everyone, any time* (default): every SoundWhacker sounds immediately with its note/chord, but the song only continues when the right one plays. |
| 🎲 **Improvise** | **Every touch** of a SoundWhacker plays **one random note** from the chosen scale – with its **own instrument**. The player decides when a note comes. **Everyone may play at any time.** Set it up under **🎲 Improvisation settings**. |

**↺ From the start** restarts the song at any time – useful when someone makes a mistake.

## Songs

The songs come from the Tischharfe app. The song list contains **all songs** – songs with **at most five different notes** are marked with a yellow **⭐** (e.g. ⭐ Ode to Joy, ⭐ Little Hans, ⭐ Jingle Bells).

- **⭐ songs:** every figure has its own note – the lowest belongs to the **red** figure, the highest to the **blue** one. All ways of playing work.
- **Songs with more than 5 notes** (e.g. All My Little Ducklings, Are You Sleeping, Brother John?, Silent Night): when the band plays, the figures take **turns** (red, purple, yellow, green, blue, red …). When playing along, **“Any SW plays melody / chords”** and **“Fixed SW order”** work; “Fixed SW notes / chords” is greyed out.

## Record your own songs

1. Choose **● Record a song**. Stage and settings disappear – only the **sound selection** and the large keyboard are visible.
2. **● Start recording** and play the melody – with the SoundWhackers (keys 1–5) **or** on the **keyboard** (G3 to C6, any note).
   **↔ Glide:** drag across the keys with the **mouse button held** or with your **finger** (iPad) – every key you glide over sounds and is recorded.
3. **■ Stop recording**. The app remembers the **order, the rhythm and how long each note was held**.
4. **▶ Listen**, **✕ Discard**, or enter a **name** and **💾 Save**.

- The notes are automatically assigned to the five figures (lowest note = red).
- With **at most five different notes** it becomes a ⭐ song. **More notes work too** – then the figures take turns.
- Saved songs appear in the song list under **“My songs”** and in the menu **💾 My songs**.

## 🎸 Record chords

Instead of a melody the band can also play **chords** – to accompany songs.

1. Choose **🎸 Record chords**. Only the **sound selection** and the chord overview are shown.
2. Give every SoundWhacker a chord: **root** (C, C#, D, Eb, E, F, F#, G, Ab, A, Bb, B) and **type** (**Major**, **Minor** or **Seventh**). At the start: SW1 C · SW2 F · SW3 G · SW4 Am · SW5 G7.
3. To try them, tap the large **coloured buttons**, touch the SoundWhackers or press keys 1–5 – the chord sounds immediately.
4. **● Start recording**, play the chord progression with SW1–5, **■ Stop recording**. While recording, the chords cannot be changed.
5. **▶ Listen**, **✕ Discard**, or enter a name and **💾 Save**.

Saved progressions appear in the song list under **“My chord progressions”** (with 🎸). Ready-made progressions to try: **Pop chords (C – G – Am – F)**, **Cadence (C – F – G7 – C)** and **Blues in C**.

**Playing:** chord progressions work just like songs – **▶ Band plays** and **🙌 Play along** with all ways of playing. The note band shows the chord names, and below the figures you can see which chord belongs to whom (e.g. “2 · Dm”).

## 🎲 Improvisation settings

In the top menu (between “🎸 Record chords” and “💾 My songs”). Here you set what sounds when you **🎲 Improvise**:

- **Key**: C, C#, D … B.
- **Scale**: Major, Minor, **Major pentatonic**, **Minor pentatonic**, **Blues** or **Oriental**. Only notes from this scale are played – so it never sounds wrong.
- **Number of notes**: 3 to 15 – how many notes of the scale are available (few = calm, many = wide range). The notes are shown below.
- **Instrument per SoundWhacker**: SW1 to SW5 each get their own instrument (e.g. marimba, flute, saxophone). Tapping the coloured button plays a test note.

Then choose **🙌 Play along** → **🎲 Improvise** and off you go.

## My songs: save, share, upload

In the top menu there is the button **💾 My songs**. It lists all your own songs and chord progressions:

- **▶ Select**, **✎ Rename**, **🗑 Delete**
- **⬇ Download**: one song as a file (JSON) – or **⬇ Download all**
- **⬆ Upload file**: e.g. on another computer in the workshop. If a song with the same name exists, the app asks: **overwrite** or **keep as a copy**.

Songs are saved **only in this browser**. To use them on another device, download the file and upload it there.

## Drums

The sound selection contains **6 drum kits**: Standard, Rock, Electronic, 808, Jazz and Brushes.

- The **SoundWhackers** then play fixed drums: **SW1 bass drum, SW2 snare, SW3 hi-hat, SW4 tom, SW5 cymbal**. This is also shown in colour below the bar.
- The **keyboard** (in “● Record a song”) is **labelled**: every key is a different drum – bass drum, snare, hi-hat, six toms, cymbals, ride, splash, tambourine, cowbell, maracas, bongos, congas and more.
- A song recorded with drums remembers the drums and is played back later as a **rhythm**.

## Noises & effects

At the bottom of the sound selection is **✨ Noises & effects** – for fun and sound stories.

- **SoundWhackers:** **SW1 phone, SW2 car brakes, SW3 horn, SW4 applause, SW5 laughter**.
- **Keyboard** (in “● Record a song”), every key labelled: phone, car brakes, car passing, car engine, horn, car crash, siren, train, helicopter, jet, spaceship, applause, laughter, heartbeat, footsteps, dog, horse, birds, bicycle bell, sea, rain, thunder, wind, bubbles, creaking door, door slam, wind chimes, laser, explosion.
- A noise always plays to the end. Touching the same SoundWhacker again restarts it.

## Together with GarageBand (backing track)

The band **always plays together with other music**: if a backing track from **GarageBand** (or Spotify, YouTube …) is running in the background on the iPad or iPhone, the band simply plays along – nothing is stopped.

- **No sound?** Then the device is **muted**: on the iPad turn off the **bell in Control Center** (crossed out), on the iPhone the **silent switch** on the side. The app shows a hint about this once, with the very first note.
- No sound after switching between apps: press a SoundWhacker once or reload the page.

## Play without internet

The app also works **offline**:

1. **Open the app once with internet.** It then saves everything on the device: all songs and all sounds (about 21 MB). Then “✓ Now playable without internet too” appears.
2. From now on the app works without Wi-Fi, e.g. in the workshop room.
3. **iPad tip:** Share → **“Add to Home Screen”** gives you your own app icon.

With internet the app automatically fetches the newest version and completes its offline storage if something is missing.

## Tips & problems

- **No sound?** Tap the screen once. Check the bell in Control Center / the silent switch and the volume.
- **SoundWhacker does not react?** Check that it is connected via Bluetooth (it works like a keyboard: SW1 = key 1).
- **For practice** set the tempo to 50–60 % and let the band play the song first.
- **The stage adapts:** it automatically becomes as tall as possible so that all five figures are visible without scrolling. On the iPhone **⛶ Full screen** works best.
